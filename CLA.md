## Authority and existing rights

You confirm that you have the authority to grant these rights, including any
necessary authorization from your employer, client, institution, or other rights

are authorized to grant. Each human author and coauthor accepts for their own work.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. The person submitting them must accept

## Acceptance

Post the exact statement provided by **CLA / signature → Details**, including
this document's SHA-256 digest, as a **new comment from your own GitHub account**
on a Nodus Research PR. A checkbox or commit sign-off is not acceptance.

The repository's public `cla-signatures` branch records your GitHub identifier
and username, statement, comment URL and timestamp, originating PR, and an exact
copy and digest of this agreement. Do not post private identity or contact data.
Acceptance is reused for covered contributions; editing or deleting your comment
does not revoke rights already granted. Different terms require new acceptance.
