Identity and independence

Legal and name notice

This notice states exactly what the names on this website identify, the project's current non-commercial status and its lack of affiliation with unrelated organisations or software.

Last updated 22 August 2026.

01 · Identity

What these names mean here.

Nodus Research identifies the personal open-source project, website and ecosystem maintained at nodusresearch.com. Nodus identifies the project's local-first desktop application.

The project was created in Spain by Jorge Pérez Burgueño. Its public identifiers are the domain nodusresearch.com, GitHub repository Drakonis96/nodus, ORCID 0000-0002-1150-1930 and conceptual DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21515531.

This website does not represent Nodus Research as a company, university department or academic research group, and it makes no claim that “Nodus” or “Nodus Research” is a registered trade mark.

02 · Independence

No affiliation or endorsement.

Nodus Research is independent. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any university, research group, company or other software project that uses “Nodus” or a similar name.

In particular, this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by UCLouvain or its separate NODUS transport-network modelling software. It is also not affiliated with or endorsed by Aalto University or any research group there using NODUS in its name.

No third-party name or mark is used to suggest origin, ownership, partnership or approval. Third-party names and marks belong to their respective holders.

03 · Current status

Personal, open source and currently non-commercial.

Nodus Research is currently a personal, non-commercial project. It sells no product or service, runs no paid plan and grants no paid access under these names. Nodus is available to download and use without charge.

This status is separate from the software licence. Nodus 4.0.0 and later are distributed under AGPL-3.0-only, which governs how recipients may use, modify and redistribute the source, including in commercial contexts.

04 · Contact

Questions about identity or rights.

For a substantiated concern about a name, mark, attribution or affiliation statement, contact the maintainer through the public GitHub profile. Requests will be reviewed in good faith.

This page is a factual project notice, not a claim about the validity or geographic scope of any party's legal rights.
