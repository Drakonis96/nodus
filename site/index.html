What Nodus is

One desktop app that takes the shape of your work.

Bring in whatever you already have — your own documents, a Zotero collection, class notes, a recorded lecture, a box of old records, a table of data — and Nodus reads it for what it actually holds, then turns it into something you can question, map and write from. Everything happens on your device.

It stays on your machine

Vaults are files on your own disk. No account, no server, no telemetry, no upload step. Backups go to a folder you choose.

Every claim keeps its evidence

Nothing is recorded without the passage behind it. Each idea, entity or finding points back to the exact page, note or second it came from.

AI on your terms

Use your own API key, or run entirely offline with Ollama and LM Studio. The model assists; it never becomes the authority on your corpus.

Free, and open to read

Nodus 4 is released under the AGPL-3.0-only licence. The whole source is public, and it will not start charging you later.

A vault is one project, and it picks a mode.

Each vault is a single file holding one body of work. Its mode decides which sections appear in the app and how the AI assistant is briefed — an academic library needs debates and citations; a class needs timetables and grades.

Underneath, every mode runs the same engine, the same private storage and the same settings. Learn one vault and you already know the others.

The four main vaults

Four ways to work. One engine underneath.

These are the modes most people start from. Each one is a full workspace with its own sections, its own vocabulary and its own live demo you can open right now in this browser.

And the rest

Other shapes the same engine takes.

Five more modes, all shipping today. They sit on the same private storage, the same settings and the same evidence rules as the four above.

Genealogy

Reconstruct family histories and individual lives from primary sources, across a tree, a timeline and a map, with GEDCOM in and out and every event tied to its evidence.

Open the demo

Worldbuilding

Build a fictional world from connected characters, places, factions, cultures, maps and histories, and plan narrative threads while the rules and continuity of the setting stay visible.

Open the demo

Primary Sources

Work critically with archival documents and manuscripts: OCR, transcription and provenance beside the original image, separating what the document says from what you infer.

Testimony

Document and analyse interviews and oral history with local transcription, speaker separation, consent and access conditions, and every passage tied to a clickable timestamp.

Prosopography

Study groups and collective trajectories through scattered sources, resolving name variants and uncertain identities without hiding the doubt behind them.
