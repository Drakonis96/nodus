# Verified projections, bounded rules and ChemFig export

## Supported contract

The model supplies only exact identities and a requested depiction/rule. It cannot supply products, atom maps, projection directions, electron arrows, TeX or validation claims. Built-in skill library version 8 migrates only untouched historical instructions, preserving user customizations.

- Fischer: open-chain aldoses with 3–8 carbons. Enumerate the bounded configurations and retain the unique scene whose complete stereochemical graph equals the reference. Horizontal bonds follow the towards-viewer convention; vertical bonds point away. [UCLA convention](https://www.chem.ucla.edu/~harding/IGOC/F/fischer_projection.html).
- Haworth: aldohexopyranoses with an exact anomer. Enumerate 32 configurations, validate a corresponding idealised 3D molfile, and project substituents above/below the ring plane. No implicit cyclization or ring opening. [UCLA convention](https://www.chem.ucla.edu/~harding/IGOC/H/haworth_projection.html).
- SN2: small neutral saturated acyclic monoalkyl halides, methyl/primary/secondary, with explicit hydroxide or iodide. Reject tertiary, strongly beta-branched, aryl, vinyl, cyclic and multifunctional substrates. Apply concerted substitution, a complete atom map, charge/atom/isotope conservation, and geometric inversion followed by CIP recomputation. This is a conditional elementary pathway, **not** an assertion of SN2 dominance over E2 or a prediction of rate/yield/conditions. [OpenStax SN2](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/11-2-the-sn2-reaction).
- Resonance: small acyclic N,N-dimethylamides with one isolated carbonyl. Generate the charge-separated contributor by changing C–N/C–O orders and N/O charges, without changing connectivity. Use a resonance arrow, not an equilibrium or temporal sequence. [OpenStax amide resonance](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/24-3-basicity-of-amines).

- Newman: ethane, propane and n-butane, with staggered/eclipsed and butane anti/gauche conformers. Fixed documented viewing axis. Six parsed ChemFig substituents reconstruct a 3D graph and dihedral; both must match the requested conformer. Arbitrary axes, numeric torsions and other molecules abstain. [OpenStax conformations](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/3-7-conformations-of-other-alkanes).
- E2: unbranched saturated acyclic C2–C6 monohalides (Cl/Br/I), explicit hydroxide/ethoxide. Enumerate anti conformers preserving input stereochemistry, verify the 180-degree H–C–C–X geometry, then derive distinct regio/E/Z products. All three electron movements and the transferred proton are mapped. No major-product ranking. [OpenStax E2](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/11-8-the-e2-reaction-and-the-deuterium-isotope-effect).
- Aldol addition: ethanal/acetone donor, methanal/ethanal/acetone acceptor and explicit hydroxide. Three independently balanced steps with matching intermediates and regenerated catalyst. Newly formed stereocentres remain unassigned; no arbitrary enantiomer or condensation/dehydration product. [OpenStax aldol](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/23-1-carbonyl-condensations-the-aldol-reaction).
- Diels–Alder: butadiene/cyclopentadiene with ethene/maleic anhydride. Deterministic suprafacial bond edits, s-cis diene, cis retention. Cyclopentadiene + maleic anhydride yields separately labelled endo/exo alternatives (or the explicitly requested approach). Independent regression identities use the full stereochemical InChIKeys of Sigma-Aldrich [endo 247634](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/aldrich/247634) and [exo 548006](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/aldrich/548006), not ambiguous common-name search hits. [OpenStax Diels–Alder](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/14-5-characteristics-of-the-diels-alder-reaction).

Other conformations, cyclic/branched E2, aldol dehydration, nitration/EAS and substituted/asymmetric Diels–Alder partners remain unsupported. Naming disagreement and unspecified input stereo still cause abstention. No embedding match can override those checks.

An execution guard rejects alternative SVG/image/legacy-TeX answers for recognized molecular drawing requests when no identity intent is supplied, dropping the accompanying unvalidated claims before any SVG repair or image-provider call. General orbital/energy diagrams remain available. This closes the E2/Newman fallback observed in live testing; keyword/markup routing is not a universal chemical-content classifier.

## Export and evidence

`chemistryScene.ts` defines explicit atoms, labels, charges, isotopes, bonds, wedges and coordinates. A narrow deterministic ChemFig serializer emits anchored branches and ring closures. A separate parser reconstructs the emitted labels, topology, orders, wedge directions and coordinates, rejects changes, and checks the canonical stereochemical graph. Projection semantics are reapplied only after the emitted geometry matches the projection scene.

The application then compiles actual ChemFig in the killable validation worker. Its hard deadline is 15 seconds for ordinary diagrams and 30 seconds for the new multi-panel rules; each individual TeX compilation remains bounded to 15 seconds. Cancellation kills the process immediately. Only successful exports receive the download button. Failure leaves the verified SVG available with an explicit export reason. The download is a **fragment**, to include in a document using the `chemfig` package.

Mechanism fragments are each round-tripped before assembly. Electron-flow endpoints are typed atom or bond indices; the SVG overlay uses measured TeX anchors, including bond centres. Persisted JSON includes molecular molfiles and roles so the complete atom map and electron-flow indices are auditable. The scheme is compiled as a whole. Atom-origin arrows do not claim to draw explicit lone-pair dots.

Multi-panel evidence is scoped per panel: alternatives are never mapped to each other. The chat displays labelled panels and offers individual exports plus a combined export when that whole compilation succeeds. Stereochemical edges are prioritized in the export spanning tree so wedges are not lost in implicit ring closures. Only used electron anchors are measured, and generated TeX lines are split to respect the bundled 5000-character input buffer. Newman uses a 0.003pt invisible axis offset to avoid PGF division by zero; its declared molecular axis remains exactly coincident in the projection.

The adapter accepts a single complete version-2 JSON intent even when surrounded by prose or identically repeated; competing intents abstain. The strict resolver still validates every field and exact user identity. Unambiguous Newman selectors (including Spanish alternada/escalonada/eclipsada) and endo/exo are completed directly from the current request if omitted, never substituted for a conflicting model field. This avoids extra inference without relaxing chemical validation.

This is not a general ChemFig parser, a proof against every collision, or an independently measured near-zero chemical error rate. The renderer/parser share a scene contract, and the chemical canonicalizer is RDKit. Compilation success alone is never chemical validation.

## Local regression checks

`node --test scripts/test-chemistry-advanced.mjs` checks enantiomeric Fischer projections, both glucose anomers, tampered ChemFig labels/angles/wedges, real projection/export compilation, opposite SN2 configurations, charge and mapping corruption, unsupported substrates, resonance and full compiled electron-flow schemes. `NODUS_ADVANCED_ARTIFACTS=1` saves inspectable SVG/JSON files under `artifacts/chat-skills/verified-advanced`.

The live harness uses `NODUS_CHEMISTRY_ADVANCED=1` and an explicitly isolated `NODUS_SKILLS_PROFILE`, selecting direct `deepseek::deepseek-v4-flash`. It checks model routing, persisted evidence, rendering and export availability. These smoke checks are separate from local chemical regressions and are not a held-out accuracy estimate.

The ChemFig download check invokes the actual button handler and compares its Blob text with the persisted validated source, intercepting the native download to avoid save dialogs or writes outside QA. `NODUS_CHEMISTRY_REPLAY` can revisit recorded conversation IDs without another inference; those replays are not new model evaluations.

The new-rule suite is `node --test scripts/test-chemistry-rules-v3.mjs`; `NODUS_RULE_ARTIFACTS=1` persists drawings and evidence under `artifacts/chat-skills/rules-v3`. It includes all six supported donor/acceptor aldol combinations, terminal C2–C6 chlorides/bromides/iodides, and the four regio/E/Z outcomes of chiral 3-bromohexane. Live mode `NODUS_CHEMISTRY_RULES=1` contains 15 distinct requests (12 supported, 3 negative). Neither suite establishes a general accuracy rate.

## New-rule results, 2026-09-08

- **61 local tests pass**, covering the new rules, previous identity/projection/renderer regressions, corrupted mappings/charges, source tampering, selector preservation and hard worker deadlines.
- The first direct `deepseek::deepseek-v4-flash` run (`rules-v3-live`) completed 14 requests: 12 passed, two failed, no renderer exceptions. Nine supported artifacts succeeded and all three negative cases abstained. Failures were an omitted Spanish Newman selector and correct repeated generic JSON embedded in contradictory prose, not incorrect certified product graphs.
- Both adapter failures were patched. A new five-request run (`rules-v3-final`) passed all five: Spanish Newman, stereochemical E2, ethanal aldol, endo Diels–Alder and butadiene/ethene Diels–Alder. This is a repair-and-retest result, **not** 14 first-attempt successes.
- Downloads were exercised through their real button handlers and checked against the persisted source. Generated Newman ChemFig and the E2/aldol/Diels–Alder SVGs were visually inspected; label clearance, electron-arrow routing and spacing between alternatives were adjusted. Combined exports label steps/approaches on the reaction arrows; chat panels have separate headings.
- The C6 boundary regression generates four distinct E2 alternatives and validates both every panel and the combined ChemFig export. Its legitimate multi-panel compilation motivated a bounded 30-second worker deadline, retaining immediate cancellation and the per-compile 15-second limit.
- The final boundary live run (`rules-v3-boundary`) passed with direct DeepSeek V4 Flash: four E2 alternatives, validated combined export, checked download payload and no renderer exception. Remote achiral chain labels were separated without moving the centres or directly attached ligands that define anti geometry; a label-distance regression prevents recurrence.
- Across the three runs, all **15 distinct requests** eventually reached the expected outcome: **12 supported artifacts and 3 appropriate abstentions**. There were two initial integration failures, repaired and retested; this is not a first-attempt or held-out accuracy claim. Final TypeScript and production build pass.
- The live profile is isolated. The database audit contained no access outside that profile. No chemical embedding A/B benefit is claimed, and these bounded examples do not establish near-infallibility for arbitrary organic chemistry.

Artifacts: `artifacts/chat-skills/rules-v3`, and `artifacts/chat-skills/advanced-chemfig/rules-v3-live` / `rules-v3-final` / `rules-v3-boundary`.

The final live negative rerun (`verified-advanced-guard`) produced two appropriate refusals, no competing visuals and no renderer exceptions. Neither interruption is counted as a completed evaluation success.
