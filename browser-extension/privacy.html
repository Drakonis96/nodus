Nodus Research Connector

Privacy

Local by design

Nodus Research Connector reads a page only after you click its toolbar icon. It sends the metadata, selected files, tags, and collection choice to the Nodus desktop application on your own computer through 127.0.0.1.

It does not run continuously, collect browsing history, use analytics, show advertising, sell data, or send captured content to Nodus developers.

If Nodus cannot download a selected attachment publicly, Chrome can ask for access to that exact website so an on-demand service worker can transfer it locally. Newly granted access is revoked when the transfer finishes. You can also clear the Nodus pairing from this extension's settings.
